## Anticipated technical challenges

- **Generalizing the controller:** `app.js` currently hardcodes Story 1 duration, audio, labels, animation arrays, and hint behavior. The safest Phase 2 change is a modest story configuration/controller layer while retaining the proven state machine.
- **Multiple discovery paths:** Three active targets could create competing hints, overlapping progress states, or accidental triggers. We need an approved rule for whether partial progress persists when the user explores another target.
- **Single global pause:** Trigger-feedback animations and their short audio cues must become pause-aware as well as the full story timelines.
- **Clean resets:** Each story needs ownership of its animations, audio, temporary SVG state, timers, progress, and focus destination.
- **Mobile audio synchronization:** Separate story tracks must not overlap or restart after Pause/Resume. Audio drift should continue to be corrected against a master visual timeline.
- **Reduced motion:** This needs designed alternatives per story, not a blanket removal of movement that erases narrative meaning.
- **SVG layering and target size:** New artwork and touch targets must fit the established composition without blocking one another.
- **Hint design:** One Story-1-specific inactivity ladder will not scale cleanly to three mysteries. A contextual or rotating hint system will need approval.
- **Deployment packaging:** The workflow currently copies exactly eight Phase 1 runtime files, so every new production asset must be deliberately added.
- **Evidence quality:** Real peer findings and physical-device results must come from you or the testers; I can structure, preserve, and analyze that evidence but cannot generate it.
